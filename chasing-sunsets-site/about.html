Why I Started

A story about hard work, family, freedom and refusing to be left behind.

Why I started Chasing Sunsets

I didn’t start this because I wanted another job.

I started because I watched ordinary people creating opportunities online, and I realised i didn't want to spend the next 5 years wondering, "What if?".

I have always worked hard. As a registered nurse, a mum of three and a military wife, responsibility and commitment have shaped my life. But I wanted that effort to build something of my own too.

I wanted more for my children and my family. More experiences. More memories. More choice. More time freedom over how we spend our time, rather than letting work decide it for us.

People say money does not make you happy. It cannot create happiness by itself, but it can remove pressure, open doors and make life feel easier. Throughout my life, whenever I've committed to something, I've given it everything and achieved far more than I ever thought possible.

I knew that if I could take the same work ethic I had always given to my career and put it into this, I could create something worthwhile.

I did not want to look back in a few years and wonder what might have happened if I had just started.

So I did.

Today

Chasing Sunsets has never just been about travel.

It is about helping people make beautiful memories and showing others that they can build something flexible, purposeful and genuinely their own.

There is no promise of overnight success. There is the possibility of growth through learning, consistency and support.
