Success Stories

Stories of confidence, connection, flexibility and progress. Individual experiences vary and are never guaranteed.

“I'm a single mum who's faced more than my fair share of challenges. I've struggled with my health, experienced burnout from nursing, lost my mum, and worried about finances. Losing my mum changed everything. Her last words were, "Go for it and make it work". I love what i'm building. It's already giving me more freedom, the opportunity to travel more, and the chance to create a more secure future for my family. But..the biggest change isn't financial, it's how much I've grown as a person. I've gained confidence, found a supportive community, and started believing in myself again.”

Maria - Single parent - Former Nurse

“After my separation, I still wanted to travel, make memories and enjoy amazing holidays. My oldest friend told me about an incredible opportunity she'd just discovered. I watched from the sidelines for a few months before finally deciding to go for it. Joining this team has genuinely changed my life. I've made real friendships, found an incredible community and I'm now building a additional income alongside my full time job because, let's be honest one income just isn't enough these days. The best part is that I now get to help other people do exactly the same. It fits around my job, children, dogs and everything else life throws at me. My only regret is that I didn't start sooner.”

Caroline - Single Parent - Tutor

“I joined this opportunity at a time when I was ready for something to change. With my son starting sixth form and my daughter at university, I finally had a little more space to focus on something for me. After years of juggling teaching and running my own business, I wanted more flexibility and something that felt exciting again. What I love most is that it's given me the confidence to start stepping away from the classroom while building an additional income for our family. It feels like something I'm in control of, and the friendships, support and community around me have made the whole journey far less daunting than doing it alone.”

Julia - Teacher & Mother
